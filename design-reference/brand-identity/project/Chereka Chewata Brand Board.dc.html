02

Construction

The mark is two circles: a full moon and an eclipsing disc offset up-and-right. Three dots orbit out of the shadow — the players, and the phone that keeps moving between them.

  • Moon radius 44u, eclipse radius 37u, offset 26u, −10u
  • Dots sized 7.5 / 4.5 / 3u — never equal, never four
  • Crescent opening always points up-right
  • Minimum digital size: 24px mark, 96px lockup

Alternate marks

Six routes off the same crescent. The orbiting-dots mark stays primary until one of these wins a real test.

Rule for every candidate: one crescent at the exact primary geometry, one added idea, nothing else. If a concept needs two added ideas to read, it's out.

A
Moon & cards
Two cards fanned out of the moon's shadow. Instantly says “game”, and the fan angle gives the mark motion. Weakest at 24px — the cards merge.

Test A and B as 60px app icons on five real phones in a dark room, then ask three people who've never seen the app what it does. 09

Mascot & illustration

Meet Chereka — the moon that already knows who the impostor is. Built from the logo's exact geometry: one crescent, two eyes, no limbs, no mouth teeth, no scary eyebrows. It reacts, it never accuses. Below are the four sanctioned expressions; anything beyond these goes to an illustrator with this sheet attached.

Ready
Home, empty states
Keeping a secret
Role reveal, pass screen
Caught
Timeout, elimination
Delighted
Wins, streaks, unlocks
ILLUSTRATION DIRECTION
  • Flat vector, 2–3 flat colors per scene, no gradients inside shapes
  • Scenes show groups, not individuals: a circle of friends around one glowing phone
  • People drawn as simple silhouettes with varied hair textures and headwear; skin tones from a warm 5-tone range
  • Light source is always the phone screen — faces lit from below
  • Environments hinted with 3 objects max: a coffee tray, a cushion, a wall lamp
  • Never: horror imagery, knives, blood, masked figures, tribal-pattern shorthand for “Ethiopian”
10

Motion & micro-interactions

Motion sells the pass-the-phone ritual. Everything is short (120–320ms), springy but never bouncy-cartoonish, and driven by the moon metaphor: things rise, eclipse and glow. Easing: cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1).

Pass hand-off
Card slides out to the side and the next player's name rises up. 280ms. Haptic tick on arrival.
Moonrise reveal
Secret role fades up behind an expanding glow, 320ms, then the glow settles. No flip, no flash.
Tap me
Press & settle
Every tappable scales to .94 in 90ms and springs back. The only universal interaction cue.
Vote sweep
Tallies fill left-to-right in sequence, 60ms stagger, so the result lands like a drumroll.

14

Accessibility

The app is played in dim rooms, passed between hands, often on cracked screens. Every rule below is a hard requirement, not a nice-to-have.

CONTRAST ON MIDNIGHT #0D0B1C
Moonlight text16.1:1 AAA
Honey text10.4:1 AAA
Mint text11.8:1 AAA
Violet tint text7.2:1 AAA
Violet 500 — large only4.7:1 AA-L
Game hues at 500 are for fills and 24px+ type. Body copy uses the 300 tint (e.g. #B8A0FF).
NEVER COLOR ALONE
Impostor · half moon
Most Likely To · crescent
Every hue is paired with a moon-phase glyph and a label, so color-blind players and greyscale screenshots still parse the game.
HANDS, EYES, SCRIPTS
  • 48×48px minimum targets; primary buttons 56px tall and thumb-reachable
  • Body text never below 15px; Ethiopic never below 16px (its counters close first)
  • Type scales to 200% without clipping — cards grow, never scroll horizontally
  • Timers announce at 30s / 10s with haptics + sound, not color alone
  • Reduce motion replaces the moonrise reveal with a 120ms cross-fade
  • All reveal screens support screen readers with role text announced once
  • Amharic never truncated mid-syllable — wrap, don't ellipsize
